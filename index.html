How troubleshooting works

The agent works like an experienced engineer on your team: it gathers evidence first, then suggests a fix. Nothing changes in your cluster without a person approving it.

  1. Receive the alarm

    Connects to Alertmanager and your existing monitoring, so every alarm starts an investigation.

  2. Gather evidence

    Collects pod events, logs, metrics and recent changes around the time of the alarm.

  3. Match your runbooks

    Searches your own runbooks and past incidents to find known causes and fixes.

  4. Suggest next steps

    Explains the likely cause in plain language with the exact commands to confirm and fix it.

Built by network and cloud engineers
